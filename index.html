The problem

Good food, wasted. Hungry neighbours, nearby.

Our solution

A bridge, not a delivery service.

AnnSetu coordinates the connection. It does not provide delivery staff or a fleet: NGOs arrange their own representatives to collect food directly from donors.

FAQ

Common questions

Who can donate?

Restaurants, hotels, canteens, event organisers, food businesses and community kitchens with surplus edible food.

Who can receive food?

Verified NGOs, shelters, community kitchens and eligible local community organisations.

Does AnnSetu provide delivery?

No. NGOs send their own representatives to collect food from the donor.

How is food safety handled?

Listings carry preparation and safe-until details, and expired food stays out of active discovery.

How does verification work?

Organisations upload documents, which are checked and then approved by a human admin.

How do donors earn certificates?

By completing confirmed donations; milestones unlock certificates and badges.

Are donations tax-deductible?

That depends on local law and the receiving organisation’s status. Please consult a tax professional.

How can NGOs request food?

Browse nearby listings, request a quantity, and coordinate pickup once the donor accepts.
